Privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026 · Applies to Requester Chat and this documentation site.

3825 IT Solutions provides Requester Chat, an integration that opens Slack direct messages from Freshservice tickets. This policy explains the information involved in that function and how to contact us. The release is currently in pre-release testing.

Information the app uses

The app reads the current Freshservice requester's email address and display name. If no requester email is supplied by Freshservice, it may use the ticket's email field. It sends the email address to Slack to find the matching active member. Slack returns member and workspace identifiers and member status information needed to validate the match and construct the link.

The app does not request Slack message-read or message-send permissions. It does not send ticket subjects, descriptions, attachments or conversation histories to Slack. It does not automatically send messages. Messages an agent writes after opening Slack are handled by Slack.

OAuth and credentials

An administrator grants users:read and users:read.email through Slack OAuth. Freshworks' platform handles the installed account's token and injects it into HTTPS API requests to Slack. App browser code does not receive or persist the token. The production integration contains publisher OAuth client credentials that are supplied privately to Freshworks; they are not part of this documentation site or its repository.

Where information is processed

Freshworks operates the app hosting, request proxy and OAuth infrastructure. Slack processes the lookup and destination conversation. Their own terms and privacy notices govern their platform processing. The app loads the Freshworks client library and Crayons UI components from Freshworks and jsDelivr, respectively. Those services receive ordinary browser request metadata such as IP address and user-agent information when resources are loaded; the app does not intentionally include requester data in those CDN requests.

If this site is published with GitHub Pages, GitHub provides the hosting and may process access logs and network information under its privacy statement. The site has no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, contact forms or payment collection. The publisher does not add cookies or browser storage to this site.

Retention and support correspondence

Requester Chat has no publisher-operated backend database or persistent identity cache. Lookup results are held in the app panel's memory while it is in use. This does not mean that Freshworks, Slack or network services keep no logs; their retention, backup and security practices are controlled by those providers.

If you contact support, we receive the email address, message and any material you choose to send. Support email is handled using Gmail. We use correspondence to respond, troubleshoot, handle privacy requests and address security or legal obligations, and keep it only as long as needed for those purposes. Please omit sensitive ticket information and credentials.

Use and disclosure

The integration uses information to locate the intended Slack member and open the conversation. We do not sell requester information, use it for advertising, or use Slack API data to train AI models. Information may be processed by the providers described above to operate the integration and support service, or disclosed where legally required.

Your choices

Your organization's administrators control installation and access. Ask them to correct requester data in Freshservice or member data in Slack. They can uninstall the Freshservice app and revoke Slack access through Slack app management. For access, correction or deletion requests involving support correspondence, contact jim.henry.allen@gmail.com. We may need to verify the requester's identity and authority. Platform account and log requests should also be directed to the relevant provider.

Security and international processing

The integration uses HTTPS requests and limits Slack scopes to member lookup. No security method is perfect. Do not use the app as an emergency communication channel. Processing locations and international-transfer arrangements depend on your Freshworks, Slack, GitHub and Google services and their agreements. No specific data-residency or regulatory certification is claimed here.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the app or its providers change. We will update the date on this page and, for material changes, use appropriate product or listing notices. Contact 3825 IT Solutions at jim.henry.allen@gmail.com.
