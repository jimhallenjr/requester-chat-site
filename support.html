Setup and support

Connect the workspace your support agents and requesters use.

Before you install

  • A Freshservice administrator who can install Marketplace apps.
  • A Slack administrator or member allowed to authorize integrations.
  • Matching requester and Slack member email addresses.
  • Agents signed in to the connected Slack workspace.

Installation

  1. When available, install Requester Chat through Freshworks Marketplace.
  2. Complete the Slack authorization in the pop-up or new browser tab. Select your organization's intended workspace.
  3. Allow the member information and email lookup permissions: users:read and users:read.email.
  4. Open a Freshservice ticket. Expand Requester Chat in Requester information or the ticket app sidebar.
  5. Select Message requester in Slack after the lookup completes. If Slack asks, open its desktop app or continue in the browser.
  6. Review the destination and compose your own message. Nothing is sent automatically.

Private development builds installed using Save and Test require dev=true in the ticket URL. Public production installations do not require development mode.

Troubleshooting

What you seeWhat to try
Install keeps loadingLook for a Slack authorization tab or browser pop-up indicator and finish the authorization.
No matching memberCompare the requester's exact email address with their active Slack member profile.
Authorization or missing permissionAsk the account administrator to reconnect Slack through app settings or reinstall the app with both required scopes.
Rate limitWait a minute and retry; avoid repeated refreshes.
Wrong workspace or default Slack channelSign in to the intended workspace, then return to the ticket and click the action again.
Freshservice unavailableReload the ticket and open the app again.

Disconnect or uninstall

Remove the app in Freshservice's app management area. To revoke Slack access, remove Requester Chat from the Slack workspace's installed apps. If you subscribed through Marketplace, also check your Freshworks subscription and cancellation controls; revoking Slack access alone may not cancel billing.
