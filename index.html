One clear action

Open Requester Chat on a ticket, wait for the email match, then select the message button.

You choose what to send

The app opens the conversation. It does not send a message or read conversation history.

One connected workspace

An administrator authorizes Slack once for the Freshservice account. Each agent uses their own Slack sign-in.

Simple monthly pricing

$5 USD per agent / month

Planned Freshworks Marketplace price. Availability, the billable agent count, taxes, billing currencies, renewals and cancellation are shown by Freshworks at checkout. Slack and Freshservice subscriptions are separate.

The app is not yet available for purchase. This site does not collect payment details.

Designed for internal support

Requester email addresses must match active members of the connected Slack workspace. Agents must have access to that workspace. External Slack Connect contacts, group chats, calling and prefilled messages are outside this release.
